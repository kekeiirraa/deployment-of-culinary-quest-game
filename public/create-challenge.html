Create a New Wellness Challenge

Design a cooking-themed wellness challenge for the community

Make it cooking-themed! Include terms like chef, kitchen, cook, culinary, market, etc.

Suggested: 10-30 for easy, 30-60 for medium, 60-100 for hard challenges

Challenge Creation Tips

  • Be specific: Clearly describe what needs to be done
  • Stay on theme: Use cooking and culinary references
  • Keep it achievable: Make sure it's realistic for most people
  • Examples:
    • Beginner: "Kitchen Prep Master – Organize your kitchen for 15 minutes" (10-30 pts)
    • Intermediate: "Chef's Workout – Exercise for 30 minutes before cooking" (30-60 pts)
    • Advanced: "Culinary Wellness Week – 7 days of healthy habits" (60-100 pts)
